Silver sheet

Every silver card has the same QR code. It opens the gift page on the homepage:

One sheet of 40 cards. Give the file to the shop and they print as many copies as they need. Nothing is saved when you download it.

Gold sheets

Every gold card has its own QR code, so each one opens a personal gift page that the sender fills in. Each sheet holds 40 cards.

Every press creates brand-new cards, and the file cannot be downloaded again. Save it safely and send it to the shop.

For the print shop

  • Paper: 13 × 19 inch sheet. Print on one side only.
  • Print at Actual size / 100%. Do not choose "fit to page" or "shrink to fit".
  • Each sheet has 5 cards across and 8 down. Every card is 2.25 × 2.25 inch after cutting.
  • Cut along the small tick marks at the sheet edges. The artwork reaches past the cut on purpose, so a slightly off cut shows no white edge.
  • The QR code is pure black on a white plate. Please do not scale, recolour or add a coating over the plate that makes it glossy.
  • Before a big run, print one sheet and scan a few cards with different phones.

Cleanup link

Gifts are deleted 7 days after they are filled in. An expired gift is removed the moment someone opens it. To clear the rest too, set your scheduler (for example cron-job.org) to open this link once a day.

The cleanup link is switched off. Set CRON_SECRET (at least 16 random characters) in your settings to turn it on.

The link only removes gifts that have already expired, so opening it more often than needed does no harm. Keep it private all the same.
